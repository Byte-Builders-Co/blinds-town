## Privacy & Light Control

### Do blackout blinds completely block sunlight?

Blackout blinds are designed to significantly reduce incoming light.

However, depending on the installation method and window configuration, small amounts of light may enter around the edges of the blind.

### Which blinds provide the most privacy?

The right option depends on your window and room requirements.

Blackout, roller, Roman, and other specialized blinds can provide different levels of privacy.

Our team can help you choose an option based on your specific needs.

### Can I control how much sunlight enters the room?

Yes. Different blind styles provide different levels of light control.

Options may include:

- Light-filtering blinds
- Sunscreen blinds
- Blackout blinds
- Adjustable blinds
- Layered window treatments
